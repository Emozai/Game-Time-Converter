3Paste the game schedule

Paste a game schedule or a TV channel listing straight from a spreadsheet, email or website. Each line needs a date (11/08/2026, 2026-11-08 or Nov 8) and a time (1:00 PM or 13:00); start and end times like 11/07/2026 01:00 GMT work too. A header such as Time (ET), or a zone written after the times (GMT, ET…), sets the time zone automatically.

Install Game Time on your phone

It opens full-screen from your home screen and works without internet.
iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Tap the ⬆︎ Share button in Safari's toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. The Game Time icon appears on your home screen.
Using Chrome on iPhone? Tap ⋯ or the share icon, then Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome / Samsung Internet)
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right in Chrome, bottom in Samsung Internet).
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install.
